# Source Register & Confidence Ratings

**Research date:** 4 September 2026

## Confidence scale
- **HIGH** — official/primary source, or the subject's own verified channel, or a first-party business listing.
- **MEDIUM** — reputable independent publication, or corroborated by ≥2 sources.
- **LOW** — single secondary/PR source, scraped directory, or self-report without corroboration.

**Hard rule: no LOW-confidence claim appears as a factual statement on the homepage or in structured data.**

## Research method & limitations

**Method.** Google Search (Turkish locale, `hl=tr&gl=tr`) via a real browser session, plus Google Business/Maps knowledge panels and public social profiles. Site-scoped (`site:`) queries were used to positively test the brief's publication claims rather than to assume them.

**Limitations, stated plainly:**
1. The general-purpose search API available in this environment is US-region-biased and returned almost nothing useful for Turkish queries. All productive research came from the browser-based Turkish-locale search. Results may therefore reflect Google's Turkish index at one moment in time.
2. Instagram and LinkedIn profile *bodies* were not read directly (login walls). Data about them comes from search-engine snippets and knowledge panels.
3. Follower counts are snapshots and are not published.
4. Absence of a result is not proof of absence — several brief-claimed publications may exist behind paywalls or may have been removed.
5. Which services Kaan performed for which client could not be established from public sources and is therefore never asserted.
