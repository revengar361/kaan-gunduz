### The five-point verification test
Every source used in this project had to satisfy **all five**:

1. **NAME** — Kaan Gündüz
2. **PROFESSION** — social media / digital marketing, *not* medicine or academia
3. **LOCATION** — Adana, specifically the Ceyhan district
4. **HANDLE** — links to or names `@kaangunduzofc`
5. **CONTEXT** — mentions local business / digital work

Sources failing any point were discarded.

### The award — handled carefully
An Instagram post on his **own official account** (~2 years before research date) reads:
> "Bu Benim İlk Ödülüm, Yılın En İyi Sosyal Medya [Uzmanı]"
referencing **"Beauty Of Turkey 2024 — Yılın En İyi Sosyal Medya Uzmanı"**.

- This is a **self-reported** award on a primary account.
- **No independent source** confirms the award exists or was conferred.
- **Decision:** no `/awards` page was built, and the award is **not stated as fact anywhere on the site**. If the client supplies a photo of the trophy, certificate or the organiser's announcement, it can be promoted to a verified credential. Tracked in `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.
