## Tier B — PR-style placements (listed with a visible label, or omitted)

These carry syndicated promotional copy. They are real URLs, but they are **not journalism** and their language must not be inherited.

| Outlet | Item | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| timeturks.com | "Kaan Gündüz: Adana'dan Dijital Dünyada Büyüyen Bir Kariyer" | Syndicated profile |
| basinhaberleri.com | "Kaan Gündüz: Sosyal Medyanın Gücünü Etkin Kullanan Bir İsim" | Press-release distribution; sole source of the 2004 birth-year claim |
| evosangels.com | "Kaan Gündüz Sosyal Medyada İzi Değil, Yolu Olan Bir İsim" | Syndicated profile |

**Language quarantine.** The following phrases appear in Tier B sources and are **banned from the site**:
- ❌ "Türkiye'nin dijital dönüşümünde önemli bir yere sahip"
- ❌ "sosyal medya sektörüne yön veren genç bir lider"
- ❌ "Türkiye'nin sosyal medya lideri"
- ❌ "Türkiye'nin dijital hafızası"
- ❌ "dünyanın dört bir yanındaki markalarla iş birliği yapıyor"

None is independently verifiable, and the last one is directly contradicted by the client roster, which is district-local. Publishing it would be the single fastest way to destroy the site's credibility with an Adana business owner.

## How /medya renders this

- Tier A items appear as full cards with outlet, date, type, neutral one-line summary and an outbound link.
- Tier B items appear in a separate, visually secondary group under an honest heading.
- No article body text is reproduced. Headline + short original summary only.
- No fabricated outlet logos. Outlet names are set in type.
