## Portfolio composition — what the roster actually proves

This is the honest strategic read, and it drives the portfolio narrative:

- **Geography:** 6 of 8 in **Ceyhan**, 1 in Seyhan/Adana centre, 1 Adana-wide. This is a *district-deep* practice, not a scattered one. Ceyhan is the real local-SEO battleground, Adana the broader one.
- **Sector spread:** food & beverage (4), grooming (1), retail/live-commerce (1), FMCG production (1), industrial B2B (1).
- **Business model spread:** dine-in venues, a producer that ships nationally, a nightly live-auction seller, and a B2B safety contractor. Four genuinely different digital problems.
- **Lifecycle spread:** an August-2025 launch (Mustafa Kebap) alongside established names (Efe Şalgamları, Sis Yangın).

**This mix is the credibility argument.** It is more persuasive than any invented metric.

Public research confirms **who these brands are**. It does **not** establish which specific services Kaan performed for each, or when.
