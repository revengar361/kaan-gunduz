2. **İLK EKRAN** — The first screen. Producing content for himself; learning the platform by shipping, not by reading about it.
3. **YEREL İŞLETME** — The local business. The turn from personal content to commercial work: a Ceyhan venue needs to be found, and posting is not the same as being found.
4. **SİSTEM** — The system. Realising that a feed, an ad account, a Google profile and a website are one mechanism, not four errands.
